Enterprise Profile

Decades of Metallurgical Mastery

Sankalp Metal is an ISO 9001:2015 certified steel manufacturing enterprise dedicated to producing dependable high-tensile metal solutions that empower critical heavy industries worldwide.

By pairing automated thermomechanical rolling mills with computerized optical spectrometry and non-destructive ultrasonic flaw detection, our production ensures zero compromise on dimensional accuracy or mechanical durability.

Our plant facilities are engineered for scalable throughput, processing over 50,000 MT annually across automotive, energy pipelines, marine structural framing, and general engineering fabrication.
