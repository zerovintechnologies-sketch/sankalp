Quality Process Architecture

6-Stage Manufacturing Pipeline

From spectroscopic chemical assaying to precision cold forming and ultrasonic certification, every manufacturing checkpoint ensures total metallurgical consistency.

01
STAGE 01 • RAW METALLURGY

Spectro Chemical Assaying

Baseline incoming billets and virgin slabs undergo Optical Emission Spectrometry to verify carbon, manganese, silicon, and trace element chemistry.

Verified to ASTM E415 Chemical Standards
02
STAGE 02 • THERMOMECHANICAL

Controlled Roll Forming

Multi-stand computerized continuous rolling mills control grain boundaries, recrystallization temperatures, and uniform thickness across the entire coil width.

Laser Gauge Profile Continuous Monitoring
03
STAGE 03 • FABRICATION

Precision Slitting & Milling

High-capacity rotary shear slitters, CNC mandrel tube formers, and automated plasma profile cutting beds size components to tight tolerances.

Tolerance Margin: ±0.02 mm
04
STAGE 04 • TESTING

Ultrasonic & Flaw Inspection

Non-destructive testing (NDT) passes all pressure pipes and heavy plates through multi-angle ultrasonic transducer scanning to eliminate internal laminations.

Level III ASNT Certified Operators
05
STAGE 05 • SURFACE

Passivation & Protective Coating

Continuous hot-dip galvanizing baths, anti-corrosion pickling & oiling, and epoxy internal linings ensure maximum service life under severe environments.

Zinc Coating from 120 gsm to 600 gsm
06
STAGE 06 • LOGISTICS

Batch Bundling & Dispatch

Corrosion-inhibiting wrapping, heavy-duty steel circumferential strapping, barcode tracking, and just-in-time dispatch across domestic and port terminals.

Global Export Packaging Standard
LAB ACCREDITATION: ISO/IEC 17025
100% Ultrasonic Tested
10,000 PSI Hydrostatic Rig
EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Reports
